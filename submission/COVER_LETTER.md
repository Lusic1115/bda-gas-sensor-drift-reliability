# Cover Letter

Dear Editors of Sensors,

Please consider our Article, “How Reliable Is Balanced Distribution Adaptation for Gas Sensor Drift? A Strict Label-Free Evaluation Across Two Long-Term Benchmarks,” for publication in Sensors.

This study evaluates the reliability of established Balanced Distribution Adaptation under a strict label-free gas-sensor drift adaptation protocol across two long-term gas-sensor benchmarks. It examines μ sensitivity, label-free model-selection risk, and dataset-dependent failure modes, with decisions frozen before target-label evaluation. It is an empirical reliability study rather than a new adaptation algorithm. The findings are bounded to the two recorded benchmark protocols.

The work fits Sensors through its focus on gas-sensor arrays, long-term drift, and the reliability of sensor-data adaptation. Reproduction code, frozen result summaries, and figures are publicly available at https://github.com/Lusic1115/bda-gas-sensor-drift-reliability; software release v1.0.0 is archived on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23610757. The reconstructed external feature representation is a study-derived artifact, not an official full-coverage processed release. The manuscript and Supplementary Materials document its provenance and scope. GenAI assistance and author verification are disclosed in the manuscript.

We confirm that neither the manuscript nor any parts of its content are currently under consideration for publication with or published in another journal.

All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to Sensors.
