# Claim boundaries

The results concern two chronologically structured gas-sensor feature benchmarks. This is a reliability/evaluation study of an established BDA implementation; it does not propose a new BDA algorithm or claim broad unsupervised-adaptation superiority. The frozen grid and tested selectors do not establish a universal winner, universal failure or safety guarantee.

The external 700-row table is a derived reconstruction from public recordings with unchanged supplied feature definitions, not an officially released complete processed table or a new dataset. The distributed 624-row omissions are temporally selective, but their historical cause is unresolved. Coverage recovery was fixed before external classification and was not performance-selected.

Oracle results are retrospective references, never deployment selectors. Uniform voting can outperform a single-candidate oracle and have negative oracle gap, but does not solve model selection. UCI present-class collapse did not replicate in the external runs; no universal collapse mechanism or physical causal mechanism is inferred. Domain counts and bootstrap/output windows remain separate; repeated temporal domains are not IID replicates. Hardware deployment and other modalities were not validated.
